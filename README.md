### Dual-Pattern Orthogonal Neuromodulation for Crohn's Disease and Endometriosis

### The Idea In One Line
Two nerves, two languages, so the signals don't get crossed.

- **Vagus nerve — Reverse Fibonacci:** 13, 8, 5, 3, 2, 1, 1 — gaps get SHORTER. A tightening call DOWN to the body to turn on anti-inflammatory repair.
- **Pelvic / sacral nerve — Forward Fibonacci:** 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13 — gaps get LONGER. A stretching lesson UP to the brain to teach the pain nerve to quiet down.

Because one tightens while the other stretches, the patterns are orthogonal. The brain can tell them apart. This is different from normal stimulators that blast one boring steady pulse until the nerve habituates and stops listening.

### Why This Might Help
- Vagus stimulation is already in trials for Crohn's / IBD via the cholinergic anti-inflammatory pathway (turns down TNF-alpha).
- Sacral neuromodulation is already used for pelvic pain.
- What is NEW here is using two opposite temporal codes at the same time to avoid crosstalk.

### What This Is NOT
- This is NOT a cure. It will not remove endometriosis lesions or heal Crohn's ulcers by itself.
- This is NOT a device. Do NOT build this and test it on yourself or anyone else.
- This is NOT medical advice. This needs lab testing, animal testing, ethics approval, and medical supervision first.

### How To Break It (Please Do)
We want you to prove us wrong:
1. Why Fibonacci? Why not any other non-linear pattern? Is the ratio special or just a good example?
2. What pulse width, current, frequency, and session length would you use? We don't have numbers yet.
3. Would the brain actually distinguish these patterns in vivo, or would they still blur?
4. Could reverse pattern cause vagal fatigue? Could forward pattern cause pelvic flare?

If you can break it, open an Issue. If you can fix it, open a Pull Request.

### How To Test It Safely
1. **Math test (done):** Simulate the patterns. Do they stay separate? See `simulation.py` and Figure 1.
2. **Cell test (needed):** Does vagus-pattern stimulation change cytokine output in immune cells in vitro?
3. **Animal test (needed):** With ethics approval only.
4. **Human test (needed):** With ethics approval, consent, and medical supervision only. Never DIY.
